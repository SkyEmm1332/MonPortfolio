How much does Titi Christian cost per month?

Titi Christian offers three plans: Startup at $2,999/month (100 hours, one active project), Scale-ups at $5,499/month (200 hours, unlimited projects), and Custom on request for enterprise teams. Yearly billing saves 15%. All plans bundle design, prototyping, and developer handoff inside one flat monthly fee.

Is Titi Christian an unlimited design subscription?

Yes Scale-ups and Custom plans include unlimited concurrent projects and unlimited revisions, capped only by your monthly hour allocation. Startup limits you to one active project at a time. Unlike pure unlimited-design models, you also get senior product designers, not just graphic-design generalists working a queue.

What is included in the $2,999 Startup plan?

100 hours per month with one senior designer plus a design lead, one active project, up to three revision rounds per piece, average 24-hour delivery, standard Slack support, and full access to UI/UX, branding, web design, and developer handoff. Best fit for pre-Series A teams.

What is included in the $5,499 Scale-ups plan?

200 hours monthly with two senior designers plus a design lead, unlimited concurrent projects, unlimited revisions, WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, design-system rollout, priority Slack support, and average 24-hour delivery. Built for Series A+ teams running product and marketing workstreams in parallel.

How does Orbix compare to other design subscription services?

Most design subscriptions ship graphic work - ads, social, marketing. Orbix ships full product work - UI/UX, design systems, Webflow builds, dashboards at the same price point. You get senior product designers, not interchangeable junior talent. Real outcomes shipped, not just queue tickets closed.

Can I pause, cancel, or downgrade my Orbix subscription?

Yes, anytime. Pause for a month, downgrade with 14 days notice, or cancel entirely with zero fees. We never lock you into annual contracts unless you choose yearly billing for the 15% discount. Your Figma files, brand assets, and code always stay yours, regardless.

Are there any hidden fees, overage charges, or setup costs?

No setup fees, no overage charges, no hidden invoices. The flat monthly rate is the rate. If scope expands beyond your hour cap, we flag it before starting the work and recommend the next tier rather than send a surprise invoice on month three.

What is the difference between yearly, quarterly, and monthly billing?

Monthly billing is the standard flat rate. Quarterly billing saves 5% Startup $2,849, Scale-ups $5,224. Yearly billing saves 15% Startup $2,549/month, Scale-ups $4,674/month. Most clients commit annually after their first successful quarter with us, not before signing on day one.

Do you offer custom enterprise pricing or one-off project quotes?

Yes, our Custom Project plan adds three senior designers, two design leads, a dedicated Slack channel, flexible hours, and priority access for enterprise scope. Book a 30-minute discovery call at skyemm1332.github.io and we will send a fully scoped quote within 48 hours, no commitment required.

Does Titi Christian offer a refund or money-back guarantee?

Yes. New engagements start with a two-week trial sprint. If the first deliverable does not meet quality or direction expectations, we redo it on us or refund the prorated amount. Our full refund policy is published at skyemm1332.github.io/refund-policy with the complete terms.
