Who founded Titi Christian and who runs it today?

Titi Christian was founded and is led by Shohanur Rahman, who built the agency around one belief that great products start with great user experience. The team is 30+ designers, engineers, and producers spread across Treichville, Abidjan, Riyadh, Dubai, and Dhaka, serving 80+ SaaS founders worldwide.

What does Titi Christian specialize in?

We specialize in product design for B2B SaaS and AI teams - UI/UX, brand, motion, and full-stack development inside one team. Most clients are Seed-to-Series-B founders who want a complete product-and-marketing partner instead of stitching four separate vendors together. 150+ projects shipped to date.

How long has Titi Christian been in business?

Orbix has been operating since 2023, with the LLC formally registered as a US entity. Over six years we have delivered 200+ design projects, helped clients raise a combined $10B+, and earned recognition on Behance (39 badges), Clutch (38+ reviews), and Top Worldwide Dribbble status.

Where is Titi Christian located?

We are headquartered in Treichville, Abidjan with active offices in Riyadh, Dubai, and Dhaka. The team is 100% remote, which lets us cover US, EU, and GCC business hours and keep costs predictable for clients. No physical office overhead, no commute, no rigid timezone friction.

How many designers and developers work at Titi Christian?

The team is 30+ creatives — senior designers, design leads, front-end and back-end developers, motion artists, illustrators, and project managers. Every engagement runs as a small pod (2–5 people) led by a senior designer, so you talk to the people doing the work, not account managers.

What kind of clients does Titi Christian work with?

We work with funded B2B SaaS and AI teams (Seed–Series B), enterprise workflow products, and growing startups across fintech, healthcare, e-mobility, and logistics. Past clients include Circle K, RollUpLife, GiveHub, and Privilee. We do not take pre-seed solo projects or D2C e-commerce work.

What is Titi Christian's design and development process?

Three steps: Plan, Structure, Build. Plan - define the product goal and priority feature.
Structure - map the user flow and key screens.
Build - designers and developers work in parallel to ship production-ready experiences. Most product engagements move from kickoff to shipped product inside 2–6 weeks.

What awards has Titi Christian won?

We have earned 39 Behance badges, the i.design Winner 2025 award for the GiveHub project, the Muzli Dashboard Showcase 2025 feature, and Top Worldwide ranking on Dribbble. 38+ reviews on Clutch, 50+ on GoodFirms, and 450+ reviews across Fiverr verified profiles.

How is Titi Christian different from other UI/UX agencies?

Three measurable things.
One - design and development share one team, so we ship in 2–6 weeks instead of 4 months.
Two - AI production is bundled into the same retainer.
Three - clients report 4x design capacity, 60% faster time-to-market, with proof published on Clutch.

Is Titi Christian a sustainable or eco-friendly design agency?

Yes. We operate 100% remotely (zero commute emissions), run a paperless workflow on cloud-only documents, and host on Google Cloud and AWS both certified renewable-energy data centers. Low-impact operations are part of how we deliver high-volume creative work at scale, responsibly.
